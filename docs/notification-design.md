# Notification delivery

Issue #141 reduces shopping interruption while preserving explicit control. The default is a 90-second fixed collection window per membership/list; a dedicated notification worker checks every 30 seconds independently of integration polling (normally delivered after 90–120 seconds). Further changes never extend that window indefinitely. Own actions do not notify their author. Each shopping item contributes its latest meaningful action, with check/undo pairs cancelling. Summaries include action counts and at most three item names. All details preserves immediate delivery; Important only limits domain pushes to assignments, personal messages and family membership changes. Category opt-outs still apply.

Optional quiet hours use the family time zone, include midnight-spanning schedules and defer even important domain updates. Automation rules remain explicitly controlled by their authors and keep their independent delivery behavior. Device permission and per-family delivery settings are separate. Disabled browser permission does not prevent editing preferences.

The database outbox survives restarts. Writers lock an existing membership before creating a unique member/resource row; schedulers lock only that outbox row, avoiding reversed lock order. Delivery rechecks active family/user, current membership (cascade removal), category opt-outs, detail mode and item privacy. Changing to Important discards nonimportant pending activity. Batches retry errors up to five times with exponential delays and stable family/resource tags. A partially successful push can be retried to another device; stable tags replace the same notification on supported clients. Delivery is best effort, not exactly once across an external push provider. Payloads clear after delivery; stale rows expire after seven days. Item names/previews are capped; no shopping notes are included. At most 128 distinct item entries plus an overflow marker are retained in one burst.

## Product evidence

Slack documents choosing all activity versus targeted activity and scheduling quiet hours: https://slack.com/help/articles/201355156-Configure-your-Slack-notifications and https://slack.com/help/articles/214908388-Pause-your-Slack-notifications . Todoist documents configurable reminder timing and notification delivery: https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/introduction-to-reminders-9PezfU . Todoist’s US App Store listing displayed 4.8/5 from about 130,000 ratings on 2026-10-09 (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/todoist-to-do-list-calendar/id572688855); reviews there specifically value configurable recurring reminders and straightforward controls. These primary sources support separate activity, cadence and schedule controls. Public app ratings describe the whole app and cannot establish that a particular notification design alone is highly rated. We therefore apply the documented patterns without inventing feature-specific ratings.

## Verification

Backend tests cover a burst, actor exclusion, cancel pairs, current preferences, revoked membership, important/all modes, Berlin quiet hours, invalid schedules/modes, persisted retry, fixed windows, independent lists, localized content and bounded failures. The integration scheduler also performs a safe fallback flush. PostgreSQL concurrency coverage checks that simultaneous writers and schedulers do not duplicate a batch. Browser coverage verifies accessible preference controls, quiet-hour editing and reload persistence.
